Available LLM Models for Semantic Operators
============================================
Costs are per 1M tokens. Latency is seconds per output token.
A call whose prompt is longer than a model's long-prompt threshold is billed at the
long-prompt rates listed under it, for the whole call.
Grouped by model family (within each family, sorted by input cost):
Within a family, the pricier model is the more capable one. Across families, prices follow each
provider's pricing and are not a ranking of quality.
